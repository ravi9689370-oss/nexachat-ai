NexaChat AI — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. What NexaChat stores (on YOUR device only)

  • Your OpenRouter API key — stored in localStorage on your device.
  • Your chat history and settings — also stored locally.
  • We collect nothing. There are no analytics, no accounts, no tracking.

2. What leaves your device

  • When you send a message, only that chat is sent to OpenRouter (openrouter.ai) to generate a reply — using the model YOU choose.
  • Your API key is sent only to OpenRouter as authentication.
  • We never see your messages, your key, or your usage.

3. Third parties

OpenRouter forwards requests to your selected model provider (e.g. DeepSeek, Meta, Google). Their handling of data is governed by OpenRouter's own policy: openrouter.ai/privacy.

4. Deleting your data

Use Settings → Saaf-safai: Saara data wipe inside the app, or just clear the app/browser storage. Everything is removed permanently from your device.

5. Contact

Original work. No accounts. No servers of ours. — NexaChat Team © 2026
